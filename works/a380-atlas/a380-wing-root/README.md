## What is drawn
- Fuselage zone: 23.6 m barrel segment (ovoid as in the barrel section), near half of the lower lobe and the near upper side cut away; far lower skin (opened below the box top across the centre box, where the far wing root passes), far upper skin to the silhouette; 11 upper frames, 8 lower frames (none across the box), stringers; main-deck floor (cut back to y −0.4) with floor beams and seat tracks; upper-deck floor (cut at y +0.6) with tracks; belly fairing flat bottom (6 panels around the bay openings) and its far side wall; keel beam (web + top flange, lit accent) between the bays; two saddles on a base plate with feet, ruler (0.5 m ticks) and label plate "A380 · WING ROOT".
- Wing box zone: centre wing box across the fuselage (lower skin, front spar, two spanwise beams, rear spar with stiffeners, upper skin cut back on the near half); left root stub to y 8.8 m: lower skin with stringers, front spar, rear spar (lit accent), three ribs with lightening holes and stiffeners, D-nose leading edge, upper skin kept only inboard of y 4.9 (cut), trailing-edge flap panel aft of 42 % RS→TE with flap lines and a spoiler beam (the panel ahead of it is cut away over the gear).
- Wing gear zone: bay far and forward walls, ceiling; one main door hinged inboard; gear rib, forward/aft pintle fittings, pintle pin, gear support beam with side-stay and actuator lugs; leg (main fitting, chrome slider, torque links), 4-wheel bogie (beam, 2 axles, 4 tyres, hubs = lit accent), folding two-arm side stay, retraction actuator on a lever above the pintle.
- Body gear zone: bay far wall, forward bulkhead (shared with the wing-gear bay), ceiling; inner and outer main doors plus an outer leg door; trunnion fittings and pin; leg (main fitting, slider, torque links), two-arm folding drag brace on aft lugs, retraction actuator from the bulkhead; 6-wheel bogie (beam, fork, 3 axles, 6 tyres with hubs), aft-axle steering actuator (lit accent) and collar.

## Interaction
Hover zones: fuselage / wing box / wing gear / body gear (readout + lit part + a small nudge). G or the "Gear up (G)" button: both gears retract/extend over 2.8 s, 8 precomputed poses (0 and 7 full detail, 1–6 coarse lathes, no hubs/links); doors sequenced: main doors open (u 0–0.18), legs swing (0.18–0.82) while the body-gear leg door closes with its leg, main doors close (0.82–1). Rest = gear down (main doors closed, leg door open). The body-gear bogie stays level, so it is drawn once and translated per pose (BWHEEL_DT, as WHEEL_DT in the nose figure); the wing-gear bogie rotates with its leg (axles tilt to vertical), so its wheels live in each pose. Click / E / Space / Enter explodes: wing stub 3.0 m outboard (wing gear rides with it), gears and doors down 0.45 m (1.9 m with gear up), upper fuselage 3.6 m up, staggered springs and view refit. Escape restores (closes explode, lowers gear). Arrows/Home/End pick zones. Idle tour. Reduced motion: no animation, states jump (checked: G → pose 7).

## Sources (verified numbers)
- Gear layout and retraction: Airbus A380 Aircraft Characteristics (AC) manual, 2-9-0: "Two Wing Landing Gears (WLG) with four wheel bogie … Two Body Landing Gears (BLG) with six wheel bogie … The wing landing gears … retract sideways towards the fuselage centerline. The body landing gears are located on the belly and retract rearward into a bay in the fuselage." https://www.airbus.com/sites/g/files/jlcbta136/files/2021-11/Airbus-Aircraft-AC-A380.pdf (opened). **Note: the brief said the body gear retracts forward/up; the AC manual says rearward, and the figure follows the manual.**
- 22 wheels = 2 nose + 2 × 4 WLG + 2 × 6 BLG; 16 braked; BLG aft axle steerable and not braked; WLG on rear spar + "Gear Rib 9 fitting", sidestay, retract actuator; BLG drag brace, steered aft axle: Airbus UK / DGLR Hamburg 2008, "A380 Landing Gear and Systems", https://www.fzt.haw-hamburg.de/pers/Scholz/dglr/hh/text_2008_06_05_LandingGear.pdf (opened).
- Main tyres 1400 × 530 R23 (Bridgestone, 40PR); nose 1270 × 455 R22: same DGLR source; 1400 × 530 R23 also in AC 2-14-0.
- WLG track 12.46 m, BLG track 5.26 m, gear stations 33.58 m (WLG) and 36.85 m (BLG) from the nose → BLG 3.27 m aft of WLG: AC manual 2-2-0 (search excerpt of the 2023 issue) and 7-2-0 footprint (12.456 m, 5.264 m, opened). Wheel spacing 1.53 m and axle pitch 1.70 m: AC 7-2-0 footprint figure (values read from extracted figure text; the label-to-dimension pairing is my reading).
- Wing root chord "limited to 60 ft (18.3 m)" and "aerodynamic optimised belly fairing (18.3 m long)": Airbus / RAeS–VDI Hamburg, Jan 2008, https://www.fzt.haw-hamburg.de/pers/Scholz/dglr/hh/text_2008_01_31_A380.pdf (opened, pdftotext).
- Fuselage 7.14 × 8.41 m: AC 2-2-0 (as in the barrel section).
