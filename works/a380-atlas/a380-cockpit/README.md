# A380 nose and flight deck — steps 1–3

Build: `node build.mjs` (dark → a380-cockpit.html) · `node build.mjs --light` (→ a380-cockpit-light.html). Numbers: `model.mjs`.
Camera: azimuth 56°, elevation 14° (same elevation as the barrel section; turned from 40° toward the cut side, because at 40° the aft end ring and rear wall hid the flight deck).
Size: 186 solids (Hero; some are repeated, e.g. seats ×2, LRUs ×18). Page about 755 KB per theme.

## What is drawn
- Hull: x = 0 (radome tip) to 9.0 m aft (cut just ahead of main-deck door 1), lofted from crown, keel and half-width tables (monotone cubic) onto the barrel ovoid of the section figure. Near (+y, right-hand) side cut away down to the gear-door line (y = +0.75 m), skin kept to the crown line. Inner and outer faces are separate solids with a computed silhouette line. 11 frames (0.6 m pitch, schematic) plus the end frame; stringers stop on the window frame lines.
- Windshield (far front pane) and two far side windows, with rims, frames and rivets.
- Radome zone: radome (drawn flat, a translucent hint), flat-plate weather radar antenna on a gimbal/drive box and arm, forward pressure bulkhead at x 2.2 m (near half cut at y +0.15) with three stiffeners and an intercostal.
- Flight deck zone (floor at 3.55 m, between main deck 2.55 and upper deck 5.30): floor beams and floor; main instrument panel with knee panel and **8 displays** (7 across: OIS, PFD, ND, EWD, ND, PFD, OIS, plus SD below the EWD), gear lever, standby display, buttons; glareshield with FCU (lit accent) and two EFIS panels; overhead panel; rudder pedals; two pilot seats on twin floor rails (pan, back, headrest, armrests), pull-out tables with KCCU keyboards; outboard consoles with side-sticks, tiller, armrests; centre pedestal with 2 MFDs, a 4-lever thrust quadrant (one lever per engine), speed brake and flap levers; 2 observer seats (centre on rails, far-side folding); rear wall (far half) with jamb and the cockpit door, open aft onto the landing.
- Nose gear zone: bay wall, roof, trunnion lug; strut (main fitting, gland, chrome slider), steering collar with two actuators, torque links, taxi light, trunnion, two-arm drag brace with lock knee, axle and fork, **two wheels** 1270 × 455 mm with hub (near hub = lit accent) and bolts; two bay doors with hinges and stiffeners.
- Aft: landing on posts and a 5-riser stair down to the main deck, main-deck floor, upper-deck floor with header beam, avionics floor with two racks of 9 LRUs each (vent slots).
- Base plate with feet, ruler (metres from the tip), label plate "A380 · SEC 11", two saddles (x 2.75 and 8.2 m).

## Interaction
Hover picks a zone (flight deck / nose gear / radome + bulkhead) → readout + lit accent + small nudge. Click, E, Space or Enter explodes: radome 1.8 m forward, flight deck 4.4 m up, gear 1.55 m down, gear doors 1.0 m down and 0.45 m out (staggered springs, view eases out to a second fit, as in the section). Escape closes. Arrow keys / Home / End pick zones. Idle tour. Reduced motion: no animation, state still toggles (checked).
Painter's order: fixed slots (base → radome → antenna → inner skin → windows/stringers → frames → bulkhead → bay wall, far door, gear, near door, bay roof → avionics → aft area → outer skin → flight deck → end frame). The flight deck group moves to a last slot once it has risen more than 1.3 m, so the lifted deck paints over the hull.

## Step 2 (gear retraction, fixes)
Backups of the step-1 sources: work/ck-build-step1.mjs, work/ck-model-step1.mjs. Size now 374 solids counted (many are hidden pose frames), page about 1.18 MB per theme (was 755 KB): the 11 gear poses cost about 40 KB each.

- **Nose gear retracts forward.** Rest = gear DOWN (axle 0.3 m below the keel line, tyres 0.5 m above the plate). The strut turns about its trunnion (x 5.65 m, z 2.25 m, a y axis) by 79°, so the wheels swing forward and up into the bay under the flight deck (axle ends at x 3.15 m, z 1.76 m). Drawn as 11 precomputed poses (`data-gear-pose` 0–10, even angle steps, the page eases the swing); poses 0 and 10 are full detail (hub bolts, link pins, taxi light), the in-between ones use coarser lathes and a faceted tyre. Each pose solves the drag brace: two side-by-side arms (y ±0.21) from fixed lugs under the bay roof (x 4.2) to the strut's forward face, with a knee that is nearly straight when down and folds downward as the gear rises. The bay was lengthened to x 2.5–6.3 m (it has to hold the swung strut), so the fwd saddle moved to x 2.2 m and the frames inside the bay now stop at its far edge.
- **Doors** hinge on their outboard edges (y ±0.745) and swing down and out (0–92°, 7 poses each): a front pair (x 2.55–4.54) and an aft pair (4.56–6.25). Sequence over u = 0 → 1 (2.5 s, linear clock): front doors open (0–0.18), gear swings up while the aft doors close on their link to the strut (0.18–0.82), front doors close (0.82–1). Extension runs the same path back: the front doors open, the gear swings down with the aft doors opening, the front doors close; so on the ground the front doors are closed and the aft doors hang open.
- **Controls:** G or the "Gear up (G)" button (top right of the card) toggles; readout says gear down / up. Escape restores rest (closes the explode AND lowers the gear). The idle tour now raises and lowers the gear once. Reduced motion jumps straight to the end pose (checked: G → pose 10, doors closed; Escape → pose 0). Explode works from both states: gear 0.4 m down from gear-down (tyres then clear the plate by 0.1 m), 1.55 m from gear-up; doors 0.6 m out, 0.3 m down.
- **Radar antenna highlight:** the dish and the near wheel hub no longer use the strip rule (solid --anatomy-lit fill, a black blob in light); they take the kit's own `[data-lit]` rule (lit-top / lit-shade paper fill with a lit edge), so the dish reads as a disc with its rings. The FCU strip keeps the strip rule.
- **Overhead panel mount:** two rails along the panel's top, each hung by two hanger links to a clip on the frames at 4.0 and 4.6 m; they rise with the flight deck, so the exploded overhead reads as a mounted ceiling module.
- **Windshield:** frame, rim and pane outlines one tone stronger; two frame posts inside the skin (x 3.2 between windshield and sliding window, x 3.98 between the side windows), glareshield height to the top.

Schematic in step 2: the whole retraction geometry (trunnion position, strut length, retract angle, brace lugs, bay length 3.8 m), door split and door angles, the door/gear timing, and the aft doors closing purely as a function of strut angle (no linkage drawn). **The near aft door is cut away** (with the near skin): hanging open beside the extended gear it hid the wheels. Door hinge fittings were dropped (the doors are single slabs with stiffener lines on the closed pose). No retraction actuator or uplock drawn.

## Step 3: slimmer page, cockpit view, near front door

- **Size**: 1,203,955 → 799,919 bytes per theme (−34 %). Two changes, no visual loss:
  - Path data is written relative (`m`/`l` in tenths, implied repeats, zero-length steps dropped) by `compact()` in build.mjs. The kit emits absolute M/L/Z only; coordinates were ~80 % of the page.
  - The wheels and axle turn about y, the same axis the strut swings about, so every pose sees them only shifted. They are drawn once (`data-wheel="far"`/`"near"`) and translated per pose by `WHEEL_DT`. The near hub bolts turn with the gear, so pose 0 and pose 10 each carry their own bolt dots (`data-bolts`), hidden in between. A pose now holds only the fork, strut, torque links, actuators and drag brace: ~10 KB, was ~38 KB. The 11 poses are kept, so the swing is as smooth as in step 2.
- **Cockpit view (V, or the "Cockpit view (V)" button next to "Gear up (G)")**: a second precomputed projection (`P_CK`, azimuth 3°, elevation 3°, see `CK`) of the flight deck alone, in `<g data-ck>` after `<g data-view>`. The two cross-fade in 0.38 s; with reduced motion the switch is instant. Content: the inner nose skin from 2.35 to 4.2 m (both sides, deck edge over the crown); both windshield panes and side windows (far side mirrored); frame posts on both sides and a centre post; floor, main panel with its 8 displays, glareshield with FCU and EFIS panels, both side consoles with side-sticks, pedestal with thrust levers, and the overhead. The seats, observer seats, rear wall and door are left out because they stand between this eye and the panel. The low elevation keeps the overhead mostly above the windshield panes; it still covers the centre post at the top. Hovering uses screen boxes per part (`CK_HOT`, smallest box wins): PFD, ND, EWD, SD, OIS, FCU, EFIS, glareshield, thrust levers, pedestal, side-stick, side console, overhead, windshield, side windows, panel. Esc or V returns. E, G, Space and Enter leave the cockpit view first, then act. The tour pauses in cockpit view and resumes 3.6 s after leaving it.
- **Near front gear door**: cut away like the near aft door, so it no longer hides the wheel mid-swing.

## Step 4: cockpit view clean-up, seat backs, wheel vs. skin edge

- **Overhead panel (cockpit view only)**: `overheadSvg(xa)` takes its forward edge; the cockpit view passes `CK_OH_XA = 4.0` (main view keeps 3.62). The panel is shorter (4.0–4.74 m) and its lower front edge sits higher, so the windshield centre post reads from the pane bottom to the crown. Rails, hangers and frame clips are unchanged, so it still hangs from the ceiling. No translucent fill was needed.
- **Pedestal (cockpit view only)**: `pedestalSvg(dz)` drops the top and everything on it (MFD slope, thrust quadrant, levers, speed brake, flap lever, seam) by `dz`; the cockpit view passes `CK_PED_DZ = 0.16` m. The top front edge is now just below the SD bezel, so the SD shows in full; the MFDs and the four thrust levers are still clear. Hover boxes follow `dz`.
- **Seat backs (cockpit view)**: `ckSeatSvg(yc)` draws each pilot seat seen from behind: base, cushion, a back cut to shoulder height with chamfered shoulders (`prismX` in the y–z plane), a headrest, two armrests and a lumbar seam. Sizes in `CKSEAT`; the headrest top is kept below the main display row, the backs sit inboard of the side-sticks. Hover: "Captain seat" (screen left, +y) and "First officer seat" (screen right, −y).
  - Handedness note: the main view looks at the +y side with the nose to the left, i.e. from port, so +y is the aircraft's left and the captain's side. The old comment on `seatSvg` said the opposite; it is corrected (no geometry changed).
- **Wheel vs. hull cut edge (main view)**: the outer skin (`HULL.outer`) is now wrapped in `<g mask="url(#nlg-wheel-hole)">`. The mask holds a black hole shaped like the two nose wheels (convex hull of both tyres' rims at pose 0, `wheelMaskPath()`); the hole carries `data-wheel="mask"`, so the page shifts it with the wheels at every pose. The skin's cut edge and silhouette now stop at the wheel outline mid-swing and with the gear up. Draw order was tried first (gear after the outer skin) but put the bay wall and strut top in front of the main-deck floor, so it was reverted.
